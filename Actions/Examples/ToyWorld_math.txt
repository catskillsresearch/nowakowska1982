**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 149–150 (worked instance)**

Take $C = \{(s,1),(s,2)\}$ with a single outcome $s$ realized only by strings
whose length equals the target time and that contain a distinguished “work”
action. Then $C$ is weakly attainable (via a length-$1$ work string) but not
strongly attainable, hence a motive conflict in the sense of §4.
