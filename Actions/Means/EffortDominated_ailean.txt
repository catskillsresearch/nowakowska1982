Effort domination (`EffortDominated`) compares equal-length strings: $u'$
effort-dominates $u$ when, at every index, the letters agree or else $u'$ has a
pause where $u$ has a non-pause.

**Theorem** (`EffortDominated.refl`). Reflexivity: equal strings dominate each
other via the left disjunct at every index.
