**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 151–152**

Among the strings that realize a goal, define *Prax* to be those that are not *effort-dominated*: no other realizer is obtained by replacing some letters of $u$ by the pause $\#$. Define *Prax min* to be the strings in Prax of minimal total duration $\varphi(u)$.
