An action system (`ActionSystem`) is a sixtuplet $\langle D,\#,\varphi,L,S,R\rangle$: an
alphabet $D$ of elementary actions with a distinguished pause $\#$; a duration
map $\varphi:D\to\mathbb{R}$ that is strictly positive on every action and
satisfies $\varphi(\#)=1$; a set $S$ of outcomes; a language $L\subseteq D^*$ of
physically admissible strings; and a causation relation $R$ relating strings,
outcomes, and discrete times. Only strings from $L$ may stand in $R$.

A pause string of length $n$ (`pauseString`) is the $n$-fold concatenation of
$\#$. A time-result (`TimeResult`) is a pair $(s,t)$ with $s\in S$ and
$t\in\mathbb{N}$. The inverse image $R^{-1}(s,t)$ (`Rinv`) is the set of strings
$u$ such that $R(u,s,t)$ holds.

**Theorem** (`Rinv_subset_L`). $R^{-1}(s,t)\subseteq L$ for every time-result.
*Proof.* Immediate from the side condition that only members of $L$ stand in $R$.

A time-result is attainable (`Attainable`) when its inverse image is nonempty.
