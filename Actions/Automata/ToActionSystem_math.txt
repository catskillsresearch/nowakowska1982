**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 169–171**

The automaton interpretation is offered as a reading of the action-system
sixtuplet: admissible strings generated by $\tau$ and $\pi$ from a start
$(u_0,b_0)$ play the role of $L$, with states as outcomes. The 1982 text
states the correspondence without a machine-checked identification of $L$.
