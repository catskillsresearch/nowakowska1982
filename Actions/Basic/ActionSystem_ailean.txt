`ActionSystem` packages the sixtuplet with positivity of $\varphi$, the
normalization $\varphi(\#)=1$, and the side condition that only strings in $L$ may
stand in $R$. `stringDuration` is the sum of elementary durations;
`pauseString n` is the $n$-fold pause, with
`stringDuration_pauseString` proving its duration equals $n$. `Rinv`
is equation (2)’s inverse image, and `Attainable` asserts nonemptiness.
