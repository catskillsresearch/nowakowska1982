**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 159–160**

**THEOREM.** Suppose that $L$ is a context free language, and that
$T\subseteq L$ satisfies condition (a). If $h(T)<\infty$, then $T$ is also a
context free language. The complete proof may be found in [5], and will be
omitted here.

**Nowakowska, Language of Motivation and Language of Actions (1973), §5.3.2, pp. 204–210**

Assume $D_+^*$ is context-free. If $H_O(\alpha,k)<\infty$, then
$O_{(\alpha,k)}$ is context-free (Thm 1): the suffix language
$C=\{uv:u\in\bar O\}$ is regular by Myhill–Nerode (finite horizon ⇒ finitely
many short non-members), and $O=D_+^*\cap C$.
