For a set $T$ of fulfilling strings, the minimal fulfilling set $\hat T$
(`HatT`) consists of those $x\in T$ that have no proper prefix still in $T$
obtained by splitting off a positive-duration suffix.

**Theorem** (`HatT.subset`). $\hat T\subseteq T$. *Proof.* Immediate from the
definition.

**Theorem** (`HatT.exists_prefix`). Every member of $T$ has a prefix in $\hat T$.
*Proof.* Induct on string length. If $x$ itself has no positive-duration proper
prefix in $T$, it lies in $\hat T$. Otherwise some proper prefix $x_1\in T$ is
shorter, and the inductive hypothesis supplies a minimal prefix of $x_1$, hence
of $x$.

The horizon of $T$ (`horizon`) is the supremum of durations of members of
$\hat T$. Finite horizon (`FiniteHorizon`) means some real $M$ bounds all those
durations.
