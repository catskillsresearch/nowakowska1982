For a set $U$ of breaking (offense) strings, the first-offense set $\hat U$
(`HatU`) consists of those $v\in U$ that are *factor-minimal*: no proper
positive-duration infix of $v$ still lies in $U$.

**Theorem** (`HatU.subset`). $\hat U\subseteq U$. *Proof.* Immediate.

**Theorem** (`HatU.exists_factor`). Every member of $U$ factors as
$x\,v\,z$ with $v\in\hat U$. *Proof.* Strong induction on length: if the string
is not factor-minimal, a strictly shorter positive-duration infix in $U$
supplies a first offense by induction.

Finite offense horizon (`FiniteOffenseHorizon`) means some real bounds all
durations of first offenses. Under a finite nonempty alphabet this implies
$\hat U$ is finite. Offense-primality (`OffensePrime`) is recorded as a 1973
side condition but is unused by the offense CF theorem.
