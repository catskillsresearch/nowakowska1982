For a set $U$ of breaking (offense) strings, the first-offense set $\hat U$
(`HatU`) consists of those $v\in U$ with no proper prefix still in $U$ obtained
by splitting off a positive-duration suffix.

**Theorem** (`HatU.subset`). $\hat U\subseteq U$. *Proof.* Immediate.

**Theorem** (`HatU.exists_prefix`). Every member of $U$ has a prefix in $\hat U$.
*Proof.* The same length induction as for minimal fulfillers: either the string
is already first-offense, or a strictly shorter prefix in $U$ supplies a first
offense by induction.

Finite offense horizon (`FiniteOffenseHorizon`) means some real bounds all
durations of first offenses. Offense-primality (`OffensePrime`) asserts that
whenever a concatenation lies in $U$, at least one of the two factors does.
