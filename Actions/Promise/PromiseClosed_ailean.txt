Promise closure (`PromiseClosed`, condition (a)) asserts that if $x\in T$ and
the concatenation $xy$ is admissible, then $xy\in T$: fulfillment is preserved
under admissible right continuation.

**Theorem** (`PromiseClosed.mono`). Closure applied to admissible $xy$ with
$x\in T$ yields $xy\in T$. *Proof.* That is the defining universal property.

**Theorem** (`PromiseClosed.eq_L_inter_suffix`). If $T\subseteq L$ is
promise-closed, then $T=L\cap C$ where $C$ is the promise suffix language of $T$.
*Proof.* Every $x\in T$ has a minimal prefix in $\hat T$, so $x\in C$ and
$x\in L$. Conversely, if $x\in L\cap C$ then $x=uv$ with $u\in\hat T\subseteq T$,
and closure upgrades $u$ along the admissible continuation $v$.

**Theorem** (`PromiseClosed.isContextFree`, positive promise CF). If
$T\subseteq L$ is promise-closed with finite horizon, $L$ is context-free, and
context-free languages are closed under intersection with regular languages,
then $T$ is context-free. *Proof.* Write $T=L\cap C$ as above; $C$ is regular by
the finite-horizon argument; the classical intersection hypothesis finishes the
proof.
