Two classical language-theoretic facts are proved for the promise and offense
arguments (formerly threaded as named hypotheses).

Regular-implies-context-free (`RegularImpliesCF`) states that every regular
language over a finite alphabet is context-free. Context-free-meet-regular
(`CFInterRegular`) states that the intersection of a context-free language with
a regular language is again context-free. Both theorems are proved in
`Actions.Promise.CFInterRegular` via the Bar-Hillel grammar × DFA product, and
are invoked by name from `PromiseClosed.isContextFree` /
`OffenseClosed.isContextFree` without remaining as open assumptions.
