Two classical hypotheses from the language-theoretic background are packaged for
the promise and offense arguments.

Regular-implies-context-free (`RegularImpliesCF`) asserts that every regular
language over the alphabet is context-free. Context-free-meet-regular
(`CFInterRegular`) asserts that the intersection of a context-free language with
a regular language is again context-free.

These are the only external language facts invoked when concluding that
promise-closed or offense-closed subsets of a context-free $L$ remain
context-free under finite horizon.
