Section 7 studies structural properties of strings that fulfill or break a
promise. Condition (a) is right-closure under admissible continuation;
$\hat T$ collects minimal fulfilling strings and $h(T)$ is their duration
horizon. Condition (b) is two-sided embedding closure for offenses, with
$\hat U$ the first offenses. (Print “is”→“if” and $T\in L$→$T\subseteq L$ are
applied editorially in the source transcription.)
