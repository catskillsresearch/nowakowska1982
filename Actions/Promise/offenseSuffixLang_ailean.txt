The offense infix envelope of $U$ (`offenseSuffixLang` / `offenseInfixLang`)
consists of all strings that contain some factor-minimal first offense from
$\hat U$ as a contiguous infix.

**Theorem** (`offenseSuffixLang.isRegular`). Under finite offense horizon
(finite nonempty alphabet), the offense infix envelope is regular. *Proof.*
$\hat U$ is finite; each fixed-infix language is regular by a finite
Myhill–Nerode partition on right takes of length at most $|v|$; a finite union
of regular languages is regular.
