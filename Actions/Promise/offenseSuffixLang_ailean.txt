The offense suffix language of $U$ (`offenseSuffixLang`) consists of all strings
that begin with some first offense from $\hat U$.

**Theorem** (`offenseSuffixLang.suffix_closed`). The language is closed under
appending an arbitrary suffix. *Proof.* As for the promise envelope: extend the
continuation after a fixed first offense.

**Theorem** (`offenseSuffixLang.none_of_long`). Under a uniform duration bound on
first offenses, strings longer than the bound and outside the language have no
extension that enters it. *Proof.* Dual to the promise case via duration
additivity.

**Theorem** (`offenseSuffixLang.isRegular`). Under finite offense horizon (finite
nonempty alphabet), the offense suffix language is regular. *Proof.* The same
Myhill–Nerode partition into universal, empty, and finitely many short
quotients.
