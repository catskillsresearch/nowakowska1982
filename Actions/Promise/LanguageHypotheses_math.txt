**Nowakowska, Language of Motivation and Language of Actions (1973), Ch. 3**

Regular languages are context-free (over a finite alphabet), and the
context-free languages are closed under intersection with regular languages.
The 1973 §5.3 arguments invoke these facts when identifying
fulfilling/breaking sets with $L\cap C$ for a regular envelope $C$. In this
formalization both facts are machine-checked Lean theorems
(`Language.IsContextFree.inter_regular` / `RegularImpliesCF`), not hypotheses.
