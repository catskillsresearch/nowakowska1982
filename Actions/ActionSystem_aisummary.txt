Nowakowska opens §2 by fixing the primitive vocabulary of the theory: elementary
actions, a distinguished pause, a duration function, the language of admissible
strings, a set of outcomes, and a causation relation linking strings to
time-results. Interpretations of admissibility (physical, psychological, social,
organizational, object-relative, methodological) are deferred; the Lean encoding
records only the formal sixtuplet and the basic lemmas about duration.
