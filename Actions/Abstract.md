# Abstract

We give a Lean 4 / Mathlib formalization of Maria Nowakowska's *A Formal
Theory of Actions* [@Nowakowska82]. The library develops the action-system
sixtuplet, weak and strong attainability (including a machine-checked 113-type
four-motive conflict taxonomy), Prax / Prax-min, promise closure, an automaton
realization of the sixtuplet, and Arrow-style choice with clinical corollary
packaging, with a small checked example.
