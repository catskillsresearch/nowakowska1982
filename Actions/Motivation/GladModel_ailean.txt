A gladness model (`GladModel`) extends an epistemic model by a gladness seed
$v_G$ disjoint from $v_T$. Gladness of $\alpha$ (`Glad`) is read as: either
$v_T\subseteq\alpha$, or else $v_G\subseteq\alpha$ together with $P(\alpha)=1$. Thus
gladness forces think-style containment or (when the gladness seed sits in
$\alpha$) knowledge.
