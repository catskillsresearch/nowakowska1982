**Nowakowska, Language of Motivation and Language of Actions (1973), §2.2.4 (c); §2.1.4.5**

Add to the epistemic model a set $v_G\subset X$ with $v_G\cap v_T=\emptyset$.
Rule (48) states
$$
G(a,P)\xrightarrow{s}K_3(a,P)\lor T_3(a,P):
$$
‘I am glad that $a$ is $P$’ implies that the speaker either knows that $a$ is
$P$, or at least thinks so.
