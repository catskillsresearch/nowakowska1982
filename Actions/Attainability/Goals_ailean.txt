`RinvUnion` / `RinvInter` implement the weak and strong realizing sets;
`WeaklyAttainable` and `StronglyAttainable` are their nonemptiness
predicates. `strongly_implies_weakly` needs a nonempty goal list.
`MotiveConflict` packages the weak-but-not-strong pattern.
`weakly_iff_strongly_singleton` shows the two notions coincide on
one-element goal sets.
