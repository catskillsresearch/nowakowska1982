Section 4 defines attainability of time-results via $R^{-1}$, then lifts Boolean
combinations of goals to unions, intersections, and complements of realizing
strings. Weak attainability is a nonempty union; strong attainability is a
nonempty intersection (correcting the printed union for “strongly”). Motive
conflict is weak-but-not-strong attainability of a desirable set $C$.
