**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 149–150**

Define the *inverse image* of a time-result by
$$
R^{-1}(s,t)=\{u\in L:u\,R\,(s,t)\}.
$$
The time-result $(s,t)$ is *attainable* when this set is nonempty. For a finite *goal set* $C=\{(s_i,t_i)\}$, say that $C$ is *weakly attainable* when $\bigcup_i R^{-1}(s_i,t_i)\neq\varnothing$, and *strongly attainable* when $\bigcap_i R^{-1}(s_i,t_i)\neq\varnothing$. Negation of $(s,t)$ corresponds to the complement $L\setminus R^{-1}(s,t)$.
