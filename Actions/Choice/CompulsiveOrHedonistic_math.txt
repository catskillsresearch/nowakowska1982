**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 154–155**

When postulate (1) (lack of dictator) is violated, the decision function is
dictatorial: one psychological continuum dominates, so the final decision is
governed by evaluations on that continuum only. Natural candidates are
Superego-typed scales (‘I must’, ‘I ought’) and Id-typed scales (‘I want’,
‘prefer’)—clinically, compulsive versus hedonistic / psychopathic extremes.
