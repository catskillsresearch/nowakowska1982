**Nowakowska, Theory of Actions (1982), pp. 152–156**

Treat evaluations on scales of motivational space as “votes” and the final
decision as social choice. Arrow’s postulates (lack of dictator, sovereignty,
consistency, independence of irrelevant options, freedom) are jointly
inconsistent. Persons whose choice functions violate lack of dictator are
described as compulsive or hedonistic according as the dominating continuum is
Superego- or Id-typed; sovereignty failure corresponds to blind obedience; IIA
failure to a neurotic all-or-nothing attitude.
