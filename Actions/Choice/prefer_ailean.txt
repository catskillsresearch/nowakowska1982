The pairwise ranking (`prefer`) that places $a$ first, $b$ second, and the
remaining option last induces a total preorder in which $a$ is strictly
preferred to $b$ whenever $a\neq b$ (`prefer.lt`). *Proof.* The rank of $a$ is
$0$ and of $b$ is $1$, so $a\le b$ holds numerically while $b\le a$ fails.
