The pure second-scale social welfare function (`scale2Dictator`) returns the
second ranking of the profile. The same arguments as for the first-scale
dictator show: the second scale is a dictator (`scale2Dictator.isDictator`);
unanimity (`scale2Dictator.unanimity`) and independence of irrelevant
alternatives (`scale2Dictator.IIA`) hold; lack of a dictator fails
(`scale2Dictator.not_lack`); and sovereignty holds
(`scale2Dictator.sovereignty`) by swapping the preferred pair in both scales.
