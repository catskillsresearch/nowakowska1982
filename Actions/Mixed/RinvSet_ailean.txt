For a set $W$ of time-results, the wanted inverse image (`RinvWanted`) is the
intersection
$$
\hat R^{-1}(W)=\bigcap_{(s,n)\in W}R^{-1}(s,n).
$$
A string $u$ belongs to this set precisely when it realizes every time-result
in $W$ (`RinvWanted.mem_iff`). When $W$ is empty the intersection is the
universe of all strings (`RinvWanted.empty`).

For a set $W'$ of unwanted time-results, the unwanted inverse image
(`RinvUnwanted`) is the union
$$
R^{-1}(W')=\bigcup_{(s,n)\in W'}R^{-1}(s,n).
$$
Thus $u$ lies in $R^{-1}(W')$ when it realizes at least one member of $W'$
(`RinvUnwanted.mem_iff`). When $W'$ is empty the union is empty
(`RinvUnwanted.empty`).
