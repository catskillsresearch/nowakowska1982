A mixed string $x$ realizes (`Realizes`) a timed sentence list
$(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_m)$ with times $(k_1,\ldots,k_m)$ when the successive
utterance occurrences of $x$ have exactly those sentences in order and exactly
those discrete utterance times.

**Theorem** (`Realizes.of_refl`). Every mixed string realizes the timed list
extracted from its own occurrences (the identity witness).

A timed sentence list is satisfiable (`Satisfiable`) when there exists a
motivationally consistent mixed string that realizes it.

**Proposition 1 (§5.3.1)** (`Satisfiable.of_motivationalConsistency`). Every
motivationally consistent mixed string determines a satisfiable utterance
system—namely the timed list of its own occurrences, with the string itself as
witness.
