A string $u$ over the action alphabet is called non-$L$-parasitic
(`NonParasitic`) when there exist strings $w_1$ and $w_2$ such that the
concatenation $w_1uw_2$ belongs to $L$. Equivalently, $u$ occurs as a contiguous
factor of some physically admissible string.

**Theorem** (`NonParasitic.of_mem_L`). Every member of $L$ is non-$L$-parasitic:
take both extensions empty.

**Theorem** (`NonParasitic.append_left_right`). More generally, if a concrete
sandwich $w_1uw_2$ already lies in $L$, those same extensions witness that $u$
is non-$L$-parasitic.
