The mixed language (`LM`) is the inverse image
$$
L_M=\pi^{-1}(L)=\{x\in(D\cup M)^*\mid \pi(x)\in L\}.
$$
Thus a mixed string is physically admissible precisely when its behavioural
projection is an admissible action string (`LM.mem_iff`).

**Theorem** (`LM.of_pure_actions`). Every purely behavioural string from $L$
embeds into $L_M$: mapping each action letter into the mixed alphabet and
applying $\pi$ recovers the original string, so membership in $L$ transfers.

A mixed string is said to contain an utterance (`hasUtterance`) when at least
one of its letters is a sentence. The domain of motivational consistency
(`LM_minus_L`) is the subset of $L_M$ consisting of those strings that contain
at least one utterance.
